Welcome to **Hungry Heart**, a vibrant and modern restaurant website designed for food lovers! We serve up a massive variety of delicious dishes in a fun, lively, and funky atmosphere.

## 🌟 Features

- **Dynamic Menu Showcase:** Explore a wide range of mouth-watering culinary categories.
- **Interactive Vibe:** Built with a funky aesthetic to give visitors a taste of our energetic offline atmosphere.
- **Responsive Design:** Looks and works great across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 🍽️ Menu Highlights

Our kitchen crafts an eclectic mix of flavors to satisfy every craving:

*   **North Indian:** Rich gravies, fragrant biryanis, and freshly baked tandoori breads.
*   **South Indian:** Crispy dosas, fluffy idlis, and authentic comforting accompaniments.
*   **Chinese Platters:** Bold flavors, sizzling stir-fries, and delectable noodle and rice combinations.
*   **Signature Nawabi Thali:** A royal spread featuring a rich assortment of our finest traditional delicacies, sweets, and accompaniments.
*   **Beverages:** Refreshing mocktails, shakes, and classic drinks to wash it all down.
